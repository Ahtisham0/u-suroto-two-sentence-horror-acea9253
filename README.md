Entry for the [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/c/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) bounty on make.fun.

## What it does

`main.ts` writes one two-sentence horror story to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly two sentences, 241 characters, well inside the 400-character gate. The program self-checks the gate (two sentences, 1–400 characters) before writing, and its output is deterministic across runs.

## The story

> My six-year-old leaves his bedroom door open a crack each night, so he can call me if he needs me. At 3 a.m. his footsteps stopped outside my door, and his small voice whispered through the gap: 'Daddy, the man in my bed won't let me sleep.'

The first sentence reads as ordinary parenting; the second reframes it — the child is not in his bed at all, and something else is.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
